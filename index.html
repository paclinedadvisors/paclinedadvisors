Sound familiar?

Programs may reach out when something in the clinical year is stretched thin.

  • Your DCE is new, or you’re about to lose one
  • A site visit or self-study is on the horizon
  • Preceptors are slipping away faster than you can replace them
  • You’re growing: a bigger cohort or a new campus
  • Your clinical team is down a person or is a little less staffed than optimal
  • Good ideas keep stalling for lack of time from the clinical team

What a project could look like

Every program is different, but here are examples of clear, finishable projects with real deliverables.

For a new or newly promoted DCE

A 90-day DCE launch plan

Weekly coaching plus the documents nobody hands a new DCE, so your clinical year doesn’t depend on what one person remembers.

You get
  • A month-by-month clinical-year calendar
  • A playbook of recurring tasks and deadlines
  • Templates for common student and preceptor issues
Before a self-study or site visit

Clinical-phase accreditation check

A close look at your clinical-year processes against the ARC-PA Standards, so gaps are fixed before they become observations.

You get
  • A B3 standards crosswalk with evidence locations
  • A prioritized gap list with fixes
  • Clear, consistent clinical-phase data for the portal
When preceptors keep leaving

Preceptor retention program

Retention beats recruiting. Build a program that makes preceptors feel valued and want to stay.

You get
  • A preceptor CME and CPAAPA endorsement process
  • Adjunct appointment and recognition pathways
  • An ongoing, routine appreciation and communication calendar
During a vacancy or leave

Interim clinical support

Steady, experienced coverage of clinical-year duties while you hire, so students and preceptors barely notice the transition.

You get
  • Rotation scheduling and placement follow-through
  • Preceptor communication and student check-ins
  • A clean handoff to your new hire
When you’re growing

Clinical capacity plan

Map exactly how many sites and preceptors a larger cohort or new campus needs, where to find them, and by when.

You get
  • A capacity model by rotation and term
  • A site development timeline
  • Options for hubs and shared placement planning
When the team is overwhelmed

Clinical workflow tune-up

A fresh look at how your clinical team spends its time, then simpler processes that give hours back every week.

You get
  • A map of current workflows and bottlenecks
  • Streamlined processes and templates
  • A short implementation plan your team can own
