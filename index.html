When programs reach out

Engagements typically begin when the clinical phase faces added demands.

  • A new or departing Director of Clinical Education
  • An upcoming self-study or site visit
  • Preceptor attrition outpacing recruitment
  • Program growth, including a larger cohort or new campus
  • A vacancy or reduced staffing on the clinical team
  • Priority initiatives delayed by limited faculty time

Example services

Representative engagements, each scoped to defined goals and deliverables.

For a new DCE or new clinical faculty

A 90-day DCE launch plan

Structured coaching, documentation, and systems that establish a sustainable clinical year that does not depend on any one individual.

Deliverables
  • A month-by-month clinical-year calendar
  • A playbook of recurring tasks and deadlines
  • Templates for common student and preceptor issues
Before a self-study or site visit

Clinical-phase accreditation check

A close look at your clinical-year processes against the ARC-PA Standards, so gaps are fixed before they become observations.

Deliverables
  • A B3 standards crosswalk with evidence locations
  • A prioritized gap list with fixes
  • Clear, consistent clinical-phase data for the portal
  • Clinical syllabi organized and aligned with the standards
Before your next clinical year starts

Category 1 CME for your preceptors

Accredited PA programs may offer AAPA Category 1 CME to their PA preceptors, yet many have not established this benefit. I manage the process from application through implementation.

Deliverables
  • An approved AAPA application
  • Objectives, activity log, evaluation, and certificate templates
  • A simple process for tracking credits and annual reporting
When preceptors keep leaving

Preceptor retention program

A structured program that recognizes preceptors and supports long-term retention of clinical sites.

Deliverables
  • A CPAAPA endorsement process
  • Adjunct appointment and recognition pathways
  • An ongoing, routine appreciation and communication calendar
During a vacancy or leave

Interim clinical support

Experienced coverage of clinical-year responsibilities during a search, ensuring continuity for students and preceptors.

Deliverables
  • Rotation scheduling and placement follow-through
  • Preceptor communication and student check-ins
  • A clean handoff to your new hire
When you’re growing

Clinical capacity plan

An assessment of the sites and preceptors required for a larger cohort or new campus, where to develop them, and on what timeline.

Deliverables
  • A capacity model by rotation, term, and cohort
  • A site development strategy and timeline
  • Options for distant clinical hubs and shared placement planning
When the team is overwhelmed

Clinical workflow tune-up

A review of how the clinical team allocates its time, followed by streamlined processes that return hours each week.

Deliverables
  • A map of current workflows and bottlenecks
  • Streamlined processes and templates
  • A short implementation plan your team can own
When a student needs extra support

Academic and professionalism remediation

Connect students who have academic or professionalism concerns with structured, supportive coaching and remediation, working alongside your faculty and following your program’s policies.

Deliverables
  • An individualized remediation plan with clear goals and timelines
  • Regular coaching sessions with progress documentation
  • Timely communication with program faculty, consistent with your policies
For DCEs and clinical faculty

One-on-one coaching

Clinical faculty roles are uniquely demanding and prone to turnover. I help navigate the “predictably unpredictable” with sites, preceptors, and students, and offer fresh approaches to stress, boundaries, and promotion and tenure. The goal is a role that is sustainable for both the faculty member and the program.

Deliverables
  • Regular coaching sessions and ad-hoc availability
  • Strategies for minor and major clinical-year disruptions
  • Promotion and tenure strategy for clinical faculty
